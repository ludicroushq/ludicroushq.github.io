Frequently asked questions

Where are photos stored?

In an encrypted app vault on your iPhone, with continuity through your private iCloud database when iCloud Drive and iCloud Keychain are available.

Does Apple Health data upload?

No. Weight samples are used in memory to draw the trend. Hey Fatty does not save them in the journal, upload them, or write to Apple Health.

Is it a subscription?

Lifetime Access is a one-time purchase. There are no recurring charges to cancel.

Do I need another account?

No Hey Fatty account. Creating or opening the private vault requires your Apple Account, iCloud Drive, and iCloud Keychain.

Can I delete my history?

Yes. Delete one check-in at a time, or permanently remove all progress photos and history from the iPhone and private iCloud vault.

Which permissions are optional?

Apple Health, notifications, and Face ID or passcode protection are optional. Camera access is required only to take a photo.

Built for a record only you can see

The joke is in the name, not in your data. Hey Fatty is published by Ludicrous LLC and designed only for iPhone. Read the complete Privacy Policy or visit Support for iCloud, purchase, permission, and deletion help.
